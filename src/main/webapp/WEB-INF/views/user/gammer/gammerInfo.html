Grammar

Parts of Speech

English words are categorized into eight parts of speech:

  • • Noun (names a person, place, thing, or idea)
  • • Pronoun (replaces a noun)
  • • Verb (shows action or state)

Grammar Examples

Noun Examples:

Pronoun Example:

"She loves her cat."

Verb Example:

"They are running to school."

Tenses

Present Tenses

  • • Simple Present: Subject + V1 (s/es)
  • • Present Continuous: Subject + am/is/are + V-ing
  • • Present Perfect: Subject + have/has + V3

Tense Examples

Simple Present:

Present Continuous:

Present Perfect:
